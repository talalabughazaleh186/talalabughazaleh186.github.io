01
Mechanical Design A · Year 2 · 2025/26

Two-Stroke Compression-Ignition
UAV Engine — Design & Thermodynamic Validation

Group 9 · 4 members · University of Birmingham Dubai

Full design and validation of a 0.76 cc, air-cooled, two-stroke diesel (CI) engine for a UAV, delivering ~45 W at 12,000 rpm for a 15-minute mission. The engine cycle was modelled thermodynamically: isentropic compression at a running compression ratio of 18:1 raises the charge from 299 K to ~951 K and 5.8 MPa; with a 1600 K peak combustion temperature the peak cylinder pressure reaches 9.76 MPa. That pressure then drives the structural design — combustion force on the piston, crankshaft bending and torsion, bearing loads and deflection checks with a safety factor ≥ 2.

Mechanical Design A · Year 2 · 2025/26

UAV Engine — Manufacturing Report
& CNC Machining of the Piston

Group 8 report + individual CAM assignment

Taking the engine from design to production for a 50,000-unit run: material screening against fatigue strength and a ≤ 10 USD/kg cost limit, selecting AISI 4340 steel for the crankcase and crankshaft, and choosing forging, CNC machining, honing, grinding and heat treatment for each part. Individually, I produced the CAM toolpaths in Fusion 360 for the Aluminium 2618-T6 piston — picked for its high-temperature strength and fatigue resistance — using 7 operations, 4 tools and a ~9 min cycle time.
