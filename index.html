Future Ocean Habitat —
Concept Design

Group D3 · 6 members · University of Birmingham Dubai

A comprehensive 204-page concept design for a fully self-sustaining underwater habitat capable of supporting human life independently from land. The habitat integrates four major mission systems: structural life support, Direct Ocean Capture (CO₂), an Underwater Data Centre, and Deep Sea Mining operations — all engineered to operate without harming the marine environment.

Future Ocean Habitat —
Mechanical Detail Design

Group DM3 · 6 members · University of Birmingham Dubai

A 128-page detailed mechanical design report taking the concept to full engineering specification. The final habitat is a pentagonal radial arrangement of geodesic pressure domes (HY-100 steel and Ti-6Al-4V) connected by cylindrical tunnels. The design achieved the highest weighted decision matrix score (4.80) out of all competing groups, excelling in maintainability, sustainability, safety, and modularity.
